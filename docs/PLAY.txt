STARFALL - START HERE

Double-click PLAY.bat or Starfall.exe to play. Keep every supplied DLL and the resources
folder beside it. Restore the complete package if a resource is missing.
No installation, account, or internet connection is needed.
Extract the complete Starfall-Windows-x64.zip before playing. The separate
Starfall-Source.zip contains rebuild inputs, not a precompiled game.
PLAY.bat --help shows launch options; PLAY.bat --check checks required files.

Detailed guides in this package:
docs/USER.md   - launching, display/sound, upgrades, saves, and recovery
docs/PLAYER.md - controls, combat, HUD, scoring, and submitting a result
docs/SAVES.md  - score compatibility, backups, and save failures

WASD / arrows: move
Mouse: aim
Hold left click / Space: fire
Shift: dash
Escape / P: pause and resume
F11: fullscreen
M / sound button: sound on or off

Survive ten waves and defeat the Dreadnought.
Cyan plus signs repair hull. Gold pickups activate overdrive.
After a run, type a name and press Save before leaving the end screen.
The top ten scores are saved on this computer.

Save folder: %APPDATA%\Starfall\Starfall\leaderboard.txt
